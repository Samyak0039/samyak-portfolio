01 / ABOUT

Designing logic.
Building digital systems.

Focused on RTL design, digital logic, and frontend VLSI development.

I’m pursuing a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Presidency University, Bengaluru. My primary interest lies in frontend VLSI design, where I use Verilog HDL to develop digital circuits, write testbenches, simulate RTL designs, and analyze waveforms.

Through hands-on projects using Xilinx Vivado and FPGA platforms, I am developing my understanding of RTL design, functional verification, synthesis, and digital hardware implementation. I aim to build a career in the semiconductor industry, specializing in frontend design and RTL development.
